# Data Sources

All documents were downloaded from each bank's own website.
Figures in this project are taken at bank level (the Kenyan banking entity),
even where the document title refers to the group.

| Bank | Document title (as published) | Period | Figures used | Source | Accessed |
|------|-------------------------------|--------|--------------|--------|----------|
| KCB | KCB Group PLC FY 2025 Audited Financial Statements | FY2025 | KCB Bank Kenya | [kcbgroup.com, Financial Statements, 2025 Q4](https://kcbgroup.com/financial-statements?years=2025&quarters=q4) | Oct 2026 |
| Equity | Audited Financial Statements and Other Disclosures | FY2025 | Equity Bank Kenya | [equitygroupholdings.com](https://equitygroupholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Equity-Group-Holdings-PLC-Audited-Financial-Statements-and-other-Disclosures-for-the-Period-Ended-31st-December-2025.pdf) | Oct 2026 |
| Stanbic | Stanbic Bank H1 2026 Financial Results (includes audited figures for the year ended 31 Dec 2025) | FY2025 (audited, as reported in the H1 2026 document) | Stanbic Bank Kenya | [stanbicbank.co.ke](https://www.stanbicbank.co.ke/static_file/Kenya/Downloadable%20files/Stanbic%20Bank%20Limited/Financials/2026/Stanbic%20Bank%20H1%202026%20Financial%20Results.pdf) | Oct 2026 |
| Co-operative | Financials 2025 | FY2025 | Co-operative Bank of Kenya | [co-opbank.co.ke](https://www.co-opbank.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CO-OP-Bank-Financials-2025.pdf) | Oct 2026 |

## Notes

- For Stanbic, only the 31 December 2025 column of the H1 2026 document is used, so that all six banks cover the same period.
- Page numbers for each figure are recorded in the KPI sheet.
